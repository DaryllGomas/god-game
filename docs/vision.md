# God Game: vision

**A small island that's yours.** You're a young god looking after one beautiful island. Everything you do leaves a mark you can see: villages grow the way you shape them, your golem grows into whoever you raised, and the land itself reflects you.

It should be **fun, sticky, and a happy place to be.** You want to spend time here for the ambience and beauty alone, and there are real challenges and stakes, but never constant stress. Black & White's balance is the model.

## Pillars

1. **A place you want to be.** Beauty and calm come first: day and night, seasons, weather, a soundscape where everything harmonises (see the calm-ambience notes in the audio code).
2. **Your touch changes the world.** Every act leaves a visible mark: villages, the golem, the land (lush and birdsong-filled if you're kind, darker and wilder if you're cruel).
3. **Stakes you care about, at your pace.** Challenges announce themselves, losses can be recovered, and the stakes are things you love.

## Three nested loops

### Moment to moment: answering prayers

Villages ask for things through soft signs at their store and totem:
- "Wood for a new house"
- "Rain, our fields are dry"
- "A child wandered into the woods"

You answer with your hands: carry a tree over, flick a rain cloud, pluck the child out of the forest. A request takes a minute or two and pays off in thanks, chimes and rising belief. This is the heartbeat of the game.

### Within a session: projects

Each village works toward something bigger: a well, a temple, a festival, and eventually a **Wonder**. Projects take several prayers plus golem help, and finishing one changes the village visibly. That's the "one more thing before I stop" hook.

### Long term: the island, then the next

Win the island's hearts and build its Wonder. Then sail to a new island with a new biome, new miracles and a new pet (the raven is a candidate).

## Challenge without stress

- **A rival god who wants hearts, not war.** A jealous god from across the sea arrives at the *end* of your first island, as a cliffhanger. Villages become a tug-of-war of belief. His moves are telegraphed (dark clouds gather a minute before his storm), and lost villages can be won back.
- **Nature as a gentle antagonist.** Seasons bring dry summers (fields need rain), autumn storms (lightning fires) and winters where food stores matter. Tending, not fighting.
- **Your golem as the wildcard.** Left alone it gets into mischief; trained well, it becomes a real helper.
- **Optional trials.** Hidden shrines with throwing challenges and delivery puzzles. Pressure only when you choose it.
- **No game over on the first island.** The worst outcome is losing villages, and you can win them back.

## What makes it sticky

- **Visible growth everywhere:** villages, the golem, the land.
- **Nested loops:** short, satisfying ones inside longer arcs.
- **Discovery:** secrets, shrines, rare creatures, hidden miracles.
- **Personality:**
  - Named villagers with tiny stories.
  - Two advisors: a kind spirit and a mischievous imp, with a little humour.
- **Gentle anticipation** ("the harvest festival is tomorrow"), never pressure tactics.
