# The Creature: design notes

**Status:** decided. The first pet is the **stone golem**; an in-game prototype is being built. Started 2026-09-29.

## Pet progression (decided 2026-09-29)

- **First pet: stone golem.** As a young god you start out working with the raw elements, so your first creature is made of earth and stone.
- **More pets unlock later.** The **raven** (your "spirit animal") is a strong candidate for a later unlock. Its notes below are kept for then.
- The golem is also the easiest to build: rigid rock parts, each pinned to a bone, so there's no skin-stretching to get wrong.

## Golem pet (in the game now)

Press **C** to find it, **L** to call it to your hand. The panel in the bottom-right shows its needs, thoughts, nature and what it has learned.

**Body.** Separate hewn-stone rocks, each attached rigidly to one "bone" (hips, legs, torso, head, shoulders, elbows, wrists), exactly how the Blender version will be rigged.

**Looks follow its own nature,** earned by its deeds rather than yours:
- **Good:** moss draped over it, little flowers, glowing teal runes and eyes.
- **Neutral:** warm grey stone, faint carved spirals, soft white eyes.
- **Evil:** glassy black obsidian, lava glowing through its joints and in thin cracks, spikes, ember-red eyes.
- The panel's "Preview looks" buttons show any of the three.

**Needs.**
- **Hunger:** rises over time; eating brings it down.
- **Energy:** drains while awake. When tired, or at night, it sits down and sleeps.
- **Growth:** every meal makes it slightly bigger (1.5× to 2.6×).

**What it decides to do on its own,** weighted by hunger and by how much it likes each option:
- Eat a food pile (a little good).
- Eat a village's ripe crops (a little bad; the villagers lose their harvest).
- Eat a rock (a golem snack; neutral).
- Eat a villager (bad).
- Pick up a boulder and throw it for fun. Bad if it lands on people, but its throws can also impress nearby villages.
- Wander, or watch your hand when it's close.

**Training.**
- **Stroke** (left-click and rub): reward.
- **Pat** (quick click): small reward, and it hops happily.
- **Slap** (right-click): punish. It flinches and drops whatever it was holding.
- Each one changes how much it likes **what it's doing right now, or did in the last 10 seconds.** A floating label tells you which lesson it took, and the panel highlights it.
  - Only the deed itself counts: picking up, winding up and throwing a rock, or grabbing and chewing food. While it's still walking over to a rock or a snack, a stroke is just affection (a ♥, no lesson). This was fixed on 2026-10-01; before that, petting it on its way to a rock taught "Throwing rocks".
- One slap teaches about as much as one full stroking session. Long rubs just make it happy.

**Left alone,** it picks up bad habits: in about ten game-minutes it drifted to −0.45 nature. You're meant to train it.

**Tuning:**
- Behaviour numbers (rates, sizes, starting opinions) are at the top of `src/creature/Golem.ts`.
- Body proportions are in `GOLEM` in `src/creature/GolemModel.ts`.

Renders: `concept/golem-prototype-neutral.png`, `-good.png` and `-evil.png`, next to `golem-chatgpt-v1.png`.

**Not done yet:**
- Learning miracles by watching you cast them.
- Leashes.
- Walking to other villages to impress them on purpose.
- Villagers reacting to it (fear or worship).

## Blender build (done 2026-10-01)

The in-game golem is now the Blender model, `public/models/golem.glb`. The code-built golem stays as an automatic fallback if the file is missing.

- **Generated by script:** `blender/build_golem.py` builds the whole golem. To regenerate after a tweak, run it inside Blender:
  `exec(open(r"C:/Users/Daryll/Documents/projects/God-Game/blender/build_golem.py").read())`
  One run builds the model, renders the three looks to `docs/concept/golem-blender-*.png`, exports the GLB, and saves `blender/golem.blend`.
- **Free tools only:** Blender plus its built-in glTF exporter. No paid services.
- **Stone:**
  - Faceted boulders: noisy icospheres with near-flat faces merged into big facets, then chamfered edges.
  - Shading between the rocks is baked into vertex colours with a Cycles ambient-occlusion bake, so crevices darken without texture files.
- **Dressing:**
  - Spiral runes and lava veins are traced across the actual rock surfaces by raycasting.
  - Moss is each rock's upward-facing skin, lifted and thickened, with five-petal flowers on top.
  - Spikes are bevelled cones.
- **Rig:** a hierarchy of joints (empties) named exactly like the code rig (`hips`, `torso`, `head`, `leg_R/L`, `knee_R/L`, `shoulder_R/L`, `upper_R/L`, `elbow_R/L`, `wrist_R/L`). Every rock is attached rigidly to one joint.
  - We skipped Rigify on purpose: a rigid golem doesn't need a deforming skeleton, and this way the game's existing procedural animation drives it unchanged.
  - Keyframed clips can still be authored in Blender later on these same joints.
- **Export layout:**
  - Rocks are merged into one mesh per joint per part (`stone_*`, `lava_*`, `rune_*`).
  - Moss caps (`moss_*`, flowers included) and spikes (`spike_*`) stay separate, so the game can grow them in and out with alignment.
  - About 16.6k triangles and 1 MB.
- **In game** (`src/creature/GolemModel.ts`, `buildFromAsset`): the file's materials are swapped by name (`Stone`, `Moss`, `Flower`, `Rune`, `Lava`, `Spike`, `Eye`, `Socket`) for game materials that recolour with the golem's nature. Eye glow halos are added in code.

Still to do: tuning the pet's mechanics after real play. (Sound landed on 2026-10-01: footsteps, purrs, slaps, crunching and snoring.)

## The raven (future unlockable pet)

The raven is the pet that fits you personally. ChatGPT picked the raven as your spirit animal from what it knows about you, and you agreed it makes sense.

Its reasoning, summarised:
- **Curious collector:** you gather fragments from very different worlds (tech, history, spirituality, markets, games, human behaviour) and try to fit them into one bigger picture.
- **Intelligence + play + experimentation:** ravens use tools, test things, remember what works, and investigate the odd object everyone else ignores.
- **Threshold creature:** ravens sit at boundaries in many traditions: old/new, spiritual/technical, human/AI.

ChatGPT's design direction: raven-*derived*, not a literal raven. Dark feathering, intelligent eyes, subtle gold or teal markings, part wanderer and part engineer. In its words, something that "found the machine in the forest and decided to figure out what it does".

## Constraints (agreed in the ChatGPT chat)

- **Free tools only:** Blender, Rigify, free animation clips (Mixamo, Quaternius). No paid services or APIs.
- **Claude builds it** in Blender by script; you don't use Blender directly.
- **Two-legged, human-style skeleton,** so free auto-rigs and ready-made animations fit.
- **Runs in real time** in Three.js (exported from Blender as GLB).
